BLD
Urgent

Severe Bleeding

Control heavy external bleeding while emergency help is being arranged.

  1. Use gloves or a barrier when available.
  2. Apply steady, firm pressure directly over the wound with clean material.
  3. Keep pressure in place and call emergency services.
  4. Do not remove an embedded object; press around it.
BRN
Prompt care

Burns and Scalds

Cool the injured area and protect it from further damage or contamination.

  1. Move away from the heat source safely.
  2. Cool the burn under clean, cool running water.
  3. Remove nearby jewellery or clothing unless stuck to the skin.
  4. Cover loosely with a clean, non-stick dressing and seek medical advice.
CHK
Emergency

Choking

Recognise a blocked airway and act quickly when the person cannot breathe or speak.

  1. Ask whether the person is choking and encourage coughing if possible.
  2. If they cannot cough, speak or breathe, call emergency services.
  3. For a conscious adult or child, use trained choking techniques.
  4. If they become unresponsive, begin CPR if trained and follow dispatcher instructions.
CPR
Emergency

Unresponsive Person

Check breathing and begin emergency care when a person does not respond normally.

  1. Check for danger, then check responsiveness and breathing.
  2. Call emergency services and request an AED.
  3. If not breathing normally, start CPR if trained.
  4. If breathing normally, place them in the recovery position when appropriate.
FRA
Assessment needed

Suspected Fracture

Reduce movement and support the injured area while waiting for professional assessment.

  1. Keep the injured person still and comfortable.
  2. Support the body part in the position found.
  3. Do not try to straighten or push a bone back into place.
  4. Seek urgent medical help, especially for severe pain or deformity.
FNT
Monitor

Fainting

Help restore blood flow safely and monitor for signs of a more serious condition.

  1. Help the person lie down in a safe place.
  2. Loosen tight clothing and allow fresh air.
  3. Check breathing and responsiveness.
  4. Call emergency services if recovery is delayed or symptoms are severe.
SEZ
Protect

Seizure

Prevent injury, track the duration and keep the airway clear after movements stop.

  1. Clear nearby hazards and protect the head with something soft.
  2. Do not restrain the person or put anything in their mouth.
  3. Time the seizure and stay with the person.
  4. Call emergency services for a prolonged, repeated or first seizure.
PSN
Emergency advice

Suspected Poisoning

Prevent further exposure and obtain immediate professional instructions.

  1. Move away from the substance only when safe.
  2. Call emergency services or your local poison centre.
  3. Do not induce vomiting unless specifically instructed.
  4. Keep the product container or substance information for responders.
HT
Cool quickly

Heat Illness

Move the person to a cooler environment and watch for altered consciousness.

  1. Move the person into shade or an air-conditioned area.
  2. Loosen excess clothing and cool the skin.
  3. Offer small sips of water only if fully alert and able to swallow.
  4. Call emergency services for confusion, collapse or suspected heatstroke.

Be prepared

Basic first aid kit

Store supplies in a dry, accessible place and check expiry dates regularly.

Dressings

Sterile gauze, adhesive bandages and wound dressings in several sizes.

Protection

Disposable gloves, hand sanitiser and a CPR face shield or barrier.

Useful Tools

Blunt-ended scissors, tweezers, safety pins and a digital thermometer.

Support Items

Roller bandages, triangular bandages, medical tape and an instant cold pack.
